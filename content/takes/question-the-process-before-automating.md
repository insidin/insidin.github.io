A process was designed for a need, within constraints that AI may have removed. Start from the need, and ask what a process addressing it would look like if we designed it today.

Automating the steps can quietly move the burden onto the people who handle the exceptions. Going from 100 to 1,000 cases a day with a 5% intervention rate still means 50 cases for a team that used to handle 20. I wrote up why this is where architecture, governance and organizational design meet.
